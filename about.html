The People Behind Nanhe Pankh

About Us

Two voices, one shared belief: every child deserves the opportunity to learn, dream and grow.

Founder

A note from the founder

“Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, dream, and grow. Nanhe Pankh was born from a simple belief — that a child’s circumstances should never decide the size of their dreams. Through education, awareness, and meaningful support, we hope to create an environment where every child can discover their potential and spread their wings.”

— Founder, Nanhe Pankh

Co-Founder

A note from the co-founder

“Nanhe Pankh is not just an initiative; it is a small step towards building a more caring and inclusive community for children. We believe that when we give children knowledge, confidence, and the right opportunities, we give them the freedom to imagine a better future. Together, we can help turn those dreams into possibilities.”

— Co-Founder, Nanhe Pankh

“We don't want to change a child's dreams; we want to give those dreams the wings they deserve.”

“Every child has potential. Our responsibility is to help them see it, nurture it, and believe in it.”
